Problem Statement

The Problem

Personal Health Management is Fragmented and High-Risk
CUREWELL // 02
CRITICAL CHALLENGE 01
📄 Paper Chaos

Handwritten prescriptions get lost, ruined, faded on thermal paper, or remain completely illegible.

  • Over 30% of physical prescriptions are misplaced within 6 months
  • Handwriting misinterpretation causes severe dosing errors
  • No centralized digital audit trail across outpatient clinics
CRITICAL CHALLENGE 02
💊 Polypharmacy Hazards

Patients taking multiple brand names with identical active molecules face accidental overdose.

  • Unintentional double-dosing of NSAIDs, Metformin, or Statins
  • Dangerous drug-to-drug interactions remain unnoticed
  • Disjointed care from different prescribing doctors
CRITICAL CHALLENGE 03
⏱️ Rushed Consultations

Patients struggle to recall symptoms, dosages, and history in hurried 5-minute doctor visits.

  • 50% of critical clinical history is forgotten under consultation stress
  • Valuable appointment time wasted trying to recall past treatments
  • Patients leave without asking critical follow-up questions
CRITICAL CHALLENGE 04
📊 Scattered Diagnostic Data

Isolated lab sheets fail to reveal longitudinal biomarker trends over months and years.

  • Incompatible formats across different diagnostic testing labs
  • Silent metabolic degradation goes undetected until emergencies
  • No visual reference ranges or unified patient timeline

Judge Defense

Frequently Asked Questions (Pre-Empting Judges)

Key Design Decisions, Safety Guardrails, and Robustness
CUREWELL // 13
SAFETY & ACCURACY
Q: How does CUREWELL prevent dangerous AI hallucinations?
A: Shifa AI is governed by deterministic domain rules and strict multi-source RAG grounded only in verified patient tables.
  • Mandatory human-in-the-loop review before any prescription enters active schedule
  • Hard-coded pharmaceutical ceiling limits
  • Model explicitly declines queries when patient data is absent rather than guessing
OFFLINE AVAILABILITY
Q: Can patients use CUREWELL during internet outages?
A: Yes, CUREWELL is an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA).
  • Active doses, emergency contacts, and active prescriptions are cached locally in IndexedDB
  • Patients can mark doses Taken/Skipped completely offline
  • Changes automatically sync with Supabase when connectivity returns
PRIVACY & COMPLIANCE
Q: How is patient health privacy guaranteed?
A: Data is protected by Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) tenant isolation.
  • No patient data is ever sold or used to train public foundation models
  • Doctor share links auto-expire via cryptographic hashes
  • Patients maintain 1-click immediate revocation rights at any time
